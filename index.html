Why We're Different

The Gold Standard in Laser Therapy

Not all lasers are created equal. Here's why Erchonia's Class 2 technology leads the world.

Our mission at Synergy Wellness is to utilize the most effective, non-invasive, causative treatments and solutions to address your health needs. We accomplish this through Erchonia's industry-leading low-level laser technology.

Erchonia is not just a brand it is the most scientifically validated laser therapy company in the world. While other companies make broad claims, Erchonia has spent decades conducting rigorous double-blind, placebo-controlled clinical trials to earn every single FDA clearance on their devices.

Class 2 low-level lasers operate at wavelengths proven to penetrate tissue and promote cellular healing at the mitochondrial level stimulating ATP production, reducing inflammation, and accelerating the body's own repair processes. No heat, no pain, no downtime.
