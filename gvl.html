What Is the Erchonia GVL?

A revolutionary dual-wavelength laser that combines two proven therapeutic frequencies into a single, synchronized treatment.

The Erchonia GVL (Green Violet Laser) is a Class 2 low-level laser that simultaneously emits two distinct wavelengths of light: green at 532 nanometers and violet at 405 nanometers. This dual-wavelength approach is what sets the GVL apart from every other low-level laser device on the market.

Traditional single-wavelength lasers target one biological pathway at a time. The GVL activates multiple pathways simultaneously, creating a synergistic effect that delivers faster, more comprehensive healing. The two frequencies complement each other in ways that cannot be replicated by applying them separately or sequentially.

Because the GVL addresses both deep-tissue repair and surface-level microbial defense at the same time, it is particularly effective for complex, chronic, or multi-layered conditions where a single treatment modality falls short.

Why Two Wavelengths?

Two Frequencies. One Powerful Outcome.

Green and violet light each activate distinct biological mechanisms. Together they address what neither wavelength can accomplish alone.

🟩
532nm
Green Light Wavelength

Green light at 532 nanometers penetrates into deeper tissue layers, reaching muscle, connective tissue, and bone. At this depth it interacts with cellular mitochondria to stimulate ATP production, reduce inflammation at the structural level, and accelerate the body's own repair cycle.

  • ✓ Stimulates mitochondrial ATP production for accelerated cellular repair
  • ✓ Reduces structural inflammation in deep muscle and connective tissue
  • ✓ Promotes nerve regeneration and improved circulation
  • ✓ Activates cytokine pathways that regulate the inflammatory response
  • ✓ Supports post-surgical tissue remodeling and strength restoration
🟦
405nm
Violet Light Wavelength

Violet light at 405 nanometers operates at a shorter wavelength that interacts with surface and near-surface tissue. It has well-documented anti-microbial properties, disrupting the cellular membranes of harmful bacteria and pathogens without damaging healthy surrounding tissue.

  • ✓ Disrupts bacterial cell membranes to reduce microbial load
  • ✓ Promotes surface-level wound healing and tissue regeneration
  • ✓ Reduces biofilm formation associated with chronic infection
  • ✓ Supports dermatological healing including skin conditions and burns
  • ✓ Enhances immune-cell activity at the site of treatment
